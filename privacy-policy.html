Privacy Policy — CMA MCQ Portal

Last updated: 24 September 2026

CMA MCQ Portal is an exam-practice application for CMA students. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address and phone number provided during registration.
  • Authentication information handled by Firebase Authentication. The app may securely store login credentials on the user's device when the user explicitly enables the “Save password” option.
  • Study and usage information such as attempts, selected tests, access/subscription status and related activity required to operate the portal.
  • Payment or transaction-related information needed to provide paid access. Payment credentials are handled by the payment method/provider used for the transaction.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide access to study material and paid features, maintain attempts and progress, provide customer support, prevent misuse, and operate the service.

Service providers

The service uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage, and GitHub Pages for delivery of the web-based portal. These providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

Data security

We use Firebase authentication and security controls to protect account information. Saved login credentials, when enabled by the user, are stored using the device's secure storage facility. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free.

Data retention and deletion

Users can request deletion of their CMA MCQ Portal account and associated personal data. Some records may be retained where required for legitimate business, legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax or accounting purposes, as described to the user when applicable.

To request account and data deletion, visit Delete Account / Data.

Children

The service is intended for students and general users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
